ScamShield AI Architecture v1.0
01

The Real-World Crisis

Victims are not deceived by bad grammar; they are exploited by targeted psychological urgency ("Digital Arrest", family emergencies, fake courier customs, high-yield task deposit schemes). By the time victims realize, funds have left via instant UPI/crypto rails.

02

Multi-Vector AI Engine

Instead of naive keyword blacklists, ScamShield uses a multi-tiered pipeline: psychological intent categorization, payment mechanics verification (e.g. reverse UPI PIN detection), and deep URL spoofing inspection.

03

Zero-Trust & Privacy

Privacy-first design: Analysis can run locally directly on client devices (Android/iOS background SMS listener or Chrome extension), meaning personal chats never need to be uploaded to cloud servers.

04

Explainable AI (XAI)

Rather than a black-box percentage, ScamShield educates the user with specific red flags, legal facts (e.g., "Digital Arrest is illegal and fictitious"), and ready-to-send de-escalation responses.
