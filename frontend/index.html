🚨 Emergency Compromise Response Playbook

Follow these critical steps immediately if you clicked a malicious link, provided OTPs, or sent funds.

1

Freeze Bank & Card Accounts

Immediately call your bank's official number (found on the back of your physical card). Request an urgent security block on unauthorized transfers.

2

Reset All Compromised Passwords

From an uncompromised device, update your email, banking, and social credentials. Enable hardware or App-based 2FA (Authenticator app instead of SMS).

3

Revoke Remote Access Tools

If instructed to install AnyDesk, TeamViewer, or QuickAssist: disconnect Wi-Fi immediately, reboot into Safe Mode, and uninstall the tools.

4

File Official Fraud Reports

Report the incident to the FTC (reportfraud.ftc.gov) or local cyber crime division (e.g., cybercrime.gov.in / IC3.gov) to establish a paper trail for disputed charges.
